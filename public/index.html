Bangalore Institute of Technology · Student Volunteer Unit

National Service Scheme (NSS)

NSS is a student-led initiative that encourages young people to take part in community service, social development and nation-building.

Our Aim

To develop the personality and character of students through voluntary community service, fostering social responsibility, leadership, discipline, empathy and a spirit of service towards society.

Core Mandate

Objectives

  1. 01 Understand the community and identify its needs and problems.
  2. 02 Develop a sense of social and civic responsibility.
  3. 03 Encourage students to participate in community development activities.
  4. 04 Promote national integration, social harmony and inclusive growth.
  5. 05 Develop leadership qualities, teamwork and organisational skills.
  6. 06 Create awareness about health, hygiene, environmental protection and education.
  7. 07 Encourage selfless service and active citizenship.

Volunteer Commitments

Roles and Responsibilities of NSS Volunteers

View activity photos

Participate actively in regular NSS activities and special camps.

Organise and support blood donation and health awareness camps.

Conduct cleanliness drives and environmental conservation activities.

Participate in tree plantation and sustainability initiatives.

Promote education and awareness among underprivileged communities.

Support rural development and community outreach programmes.

Assist in disaster relief and emergency response activities.

Encourage social awareness, equality and community participation.

Maintain discipline, teamwork and dedication during all NSS programmes.
